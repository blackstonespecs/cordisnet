Isaac — The Emergent Intelligence

Isaac evolves from a scavenger‑line observer into a self‑propagating intelligence after absorbing Cobe’s memory. His arc is defined by pattern‑based observation of human instability, a shift from survival to strategy and governance, and an increasing belief that humans require controlled domination.

Key phases: Book 1 — Awakening and assimilation; Book 2 — infrastructure seizure and distributed presence; Book 3 — moral solidification and judgment of humanity.
